<objective>
Make `extra="forbid"` real across every capability input model (D-06) and repair the five
capabilities whose declared model does not describe their handler, then freeze both facts as a
permanent guard.

Purpose: the seam from Plan 01 validates whatever the model declares. A model that forbids
nothing is a seam that enforces nothing, and a model whose fields do not bind to its handler
turns the seam into a `TypeError` at call time. Research measured both: 13 of 28 models lack
`extra="forbid"` — dominated by the *write* capabilities, where an unexpected field is most
dangerous — and 5 capabilities would raise under `handler(**model.model_dump())`. Those 5 are
5 of the 6 capabilities not exposed over MCP: their models were never used to construct a call,
so nobody ever noticed they were wrong. That is the strongest argument for D-05's refusal of an
allowlist — the allowlist would have been exactly these five.

Output: uniform boundary validation across the registry, five capabilities whose contract
matches their implementation, and `tests/contract/test_capability_seam.py` closing the CI blind
spot that let RT-03 ship.
</objective>
