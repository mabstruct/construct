<objective>
Conform the views contract models to the bytes the generator actually writes, pick and apply a
single canonical shape for `events.json`, and close the two files that have no contract model at
all.

Purpose: `views validate` rejects 5 of the 8 files `views generate` writes on a populated install
root, and 4 of those 5 fail with missing-required-field errors caused by field renames — which
D-03's ignore-extra relaxation does not fix. The generator computes a correctly-adapted
projection and then writes the raw parser dict, so the writer and the reader have forked inside
one command pair. This plan fixes the reader half.

Output: 13 conformed models plus 2 new ones, one canonical event shape, a `DigestRecord` whose
two authors agree, and a contract test proving the relaxation did not turn the models into
"accept anything".
</objective>

**The named decision this plan carries (D-01), and why it is not a reversal of ING-02.**
PROJECT.md's standing ING-02 decision is "conform the data to the gate, not weaken the gate".
This plan does the opposite for views, and that is correct rather than contradictory: ING-02
governs **canonical truth** (cards, refs, connections), where strictness protects the knowledge
model. Views is a **derived projection** whose author is `views/generate.py` and whose consumer
is the SPA — there the written bytes *are* the de-facto contract, and the models were simply
transcribed wrongly. Record this reasoning as a `# D-01:` comment block in the `views/models.py`
module docstring, in the same style the already-conformed `DomainRecord` uses at `:109`, so a
future reviewer does not "restore convention".

**AGENTS.md tension, resolved by decision.** AGENTS.md § Pydantic Conventions mandates
`ConfigDict(extra="forbid")` on every model. D-03 relaxes the views models to ignore-extra. This
is a deliberate, user-locked exception scoped to the derived projection; Plan 01 adds the
corresponding note to AGENTS.md. Do **not** harmonize this with D-06, which applies forbid across
all capability models — views is a projection, capabilities are a trust boundary, and the
difference is the reason both are correct.

**Corrections that supersede CONTEXT.md prose.** D-01's justification "the SPA already reads
these bytes" is verified true for cards and digests and **false for events** — four mutually
incompatible event shapes exist (research Finding V3). D-17 resolves that by picking the Python
emitter's shape as canonical. D-03 alone does not fix VFIX-01: research measured 5 of 8 files
failing on a populated root, 4 of them from renames (D-20).

**Two different classes are named `EventRecord`** (research Pitfall 8):
`construct.schemas.config.EventRecord` is the emitter's model (`ts`, `agent`, `action`, `target`,
`detail`, `result`) and `construct.views.models.EventRecord` is the views validator's. They share
a name and no fields. Import qualified in every diff in this plan.
