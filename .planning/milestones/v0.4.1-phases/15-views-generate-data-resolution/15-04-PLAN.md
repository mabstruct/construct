<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| workflow layer → views layer | Three new call sites cross from `llm/` into `views/`, triggering filesystem writes as a side effect of a workflow completing. |
| workflow config → refresh gating | Config values decide whether a filesystem-writing sweep runs unattended at the tail of every workflow. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-12 | Denial of Service | three refresh sweeps per daily cycle writing under `views/build/` | medium | mitigate | An unbounded full regeneration at the tail of every workflow could dominate a daily cycle's runtime on a large install root. Mitigated by the existing incremental fingerprinting (`views/lib/fingerprint.py` and the `old_ws_fps`/`changed_ws` pass), which makes the second and third sweeps near-no-ops — and Task 3 asserts that claim with a test rather than assuming it. The `views.auto_regenerate` gate provides an operator kill switch. |
| T-15-13 | Tampering | `refresh_views` broad exception handler | medium | mitigate | A swallow-everything handler can hide a genuine, repeated failure indefinitely. Mitigated by requiring every failure to be logged through the error-sanitising helper with the outcome reason preserved, and by returning a structured failed outcome that the three call sites log rather than discard. Deliberately does not escalate: D-12 makes the refresh a side effect, never a success condition. |
| T-15-14 | Repudiation | replacing a fabricated skipped step result with a real one | high | mitigate | The node being replaced reported a hardcoded status detached from what actually happened — an audit trail that lies. Mitigated by deriving the step result from the helper's real outcome and by the acceptance criteria asserting the placeholder helper and its stale reason string are both absent from the file. |
| T-15-15 | Information Disclosure | refresh failure reasons in workflow logs and step results | low | accept | Reasons can embed workspace file paths. Local-first single-user; routed through the existing sanitising helper, consistent with every other node in these modules. |

ASVS level 1; blocking threshold `high`. T-15-14 is mitigated with automated criteria in this plan.
T-15-12 and T-15-13 are medium, below the blocking threshold, and both carry an automated check.
